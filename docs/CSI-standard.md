## What is CSI Format?

**CSI** stands for the **Construction Specifications Institute**, which is a professional association that develops standards to improve communication and documentation in the construction industry. The CSI MasterFormat is their flagship standard.

## CSI MasterFormat Structure

The **CSI MasterFormat** is a comprehensive classification system that organizes construction information into **50 divisions**, each representing a specific category of construction work. The format uses a hierarchical numbering system:

### Format Pattern
- **Pattern**: `## ## ##` (e.g., "14 24 00")
- **Structure**: Division - Section - Subsection

### Example from Your Documents
In your hydraulic elevator specification, **"14 24 00"** means:
- **14** = Division 14 (Conveying Equipment)
- **24** = Section 24 (Hydraulic Elevators) 
- **00** = Subsection 00 (General)

## Why It's Important

1. **Industry Standard**: Used across North America and recognized internationally
2. **Communication Tool**: Provides common language for architects, engineers, contractors, and suppliers
3. **Organization**: Systematically organizes project manuals, specifications, and cost estimates
4. **Efficiency**: Reduces misunderstandings and improves project coordination
